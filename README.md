## 🕊️ The Experience

Designed to capture the grace, warmth, and opulence of traditional Indian wedding ceremonies, this digital invitation unfolds like an heirloom royal letterpress card.

* **💌 The Royal Envelope & Wax Seal**  
  A rich maroon paper envelope embossed with traditional botanical motifs and sealed with a bespoke **"AA" Gold Wax Seal**. A single gentle tap breaks the seal, parting the flap to release a burst of celestial golden light and unveil the sacred invitation tucked inside.

* **🎶 Melodies of Celebration**  
  As the invitation opens, gentle classical shehnai and instrumental melodies fill the air, accompanied by delicate haptic feedback and ambient chimes that transport guests straight into the celebration.

* **✨ Sacred Ceremonies**  
  Each ceremony is presented as a living illustrated chapter:
  * **Tilak & Sangeet Ceremony** — *1st December, 2026*  
    An evening of traditional blessings, dance, and music under glowing crystal chandeliers.
  * **The Sacred Wedding** — *4th December, 2026*  
    Sacred Vedic vows, the seven pheras, and eternal promises beneath a floral mandap as rose petals gently fall from the heavens.

* **💫 Interactive Save the Date**  
  Guests are invited to scratch golden foil hearts to reveal the sacred wedding date (*04 · December · 2026*), accompanied by a real-time countdown to the big day.

* **🌸 A Glimpse of Us**  
  A curated gallery of memories, preserving tender moments and shared laughter along the journey to forever.

* **🎉 Couple Trivia & Blessings**  
  A playful, heartfelt 5-question trivia game letting family and friends celebrate how well they know the bride and groom before leaving their blessings.
